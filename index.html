Our Story

Made with Passion

Green Apple's Food & Catering is a beloved culinary destination nestled in the heart of Ondipudur, Coimbatore. With an impressive rating of 4.9 out of 5 from over 218 satisfied customers, we have built a strong reputation for delivering fresh, flavorful, and hygienically prepared food that feels just like home. Whether you need a quick bite or a full catering spread, we are here to serve you with a smile.

Located conveniently behind the Post Office in Shanmuga Layout, our establishment operates virtually round the clock, six days a week, ensuring that your hunger pangs are never left unanswered. From intimate family gatherings to large corporate events, our experienced culinary team crafts every dish with love, quality ingredients, and authentic Tamil flavors. At Green Apple's, we don't just serve food — we create memorable dining experiences.
